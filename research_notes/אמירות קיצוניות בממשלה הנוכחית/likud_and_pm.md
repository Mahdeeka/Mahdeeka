# Extreme / dehumanizing public statements by Likud ministers, MKs and the Prime Minister (Netanyahu government, Dec 2022 – Nov 2026)

Research notes, compiled 22 Nov 2026. Each entry is marked with a **verification level**:
- **[V1] Primary/near-primary**: the text comes from a document that quotes the original post or broadcast with its link (mainly the statement list with Hebrew originals that Al-Haq published on 10 Jan 2024, ahead of the ICJ hearings, which carries the original tweet URLs), plus a mainstream Israeli outlet.
- **[V2] Reliable secondary**: Times of Israel, Jerusalem Post, Ynet, Middle East Eye or similar. No primary text or Hebrew obtained.
- **[V3] Weak or unconfirmed**: advocacy outlets or Wikipedia mirrors only. Verify before publishing.

**Hebrew-text caveat:** the Hebrew originals below were rebuilt from a PDF text extraction of the Al-Haq list ([Al-Haq, "Statements by Israeli Officials, Soldiers and Civil Society on Genocide", 10 Jan 2024](https://www.alhaq.org/cached_uploads/download/2024/01/10/official-translation-of-statements-informal-translations-final-list-1704917626.pdf)). The extraction scrambles right-to-left segment order, and I had to reassemble them. The wording should be right, but punctuation and segment order must be checked against the original tweet or recording (tweet URLs are given; some posts may since have been deleted) before anything is quoted in court or in print.

## 1. Entry-by-entry database: exact words, date, platform, context, consequence

### Takeaway
The core, best-documented set of statements dates from Oct 2023 to Jan 2024. These include Netanyahu (Amalek), Gallant ("human animals"/complete siege), Katz (no water/electricity), Dichter ("Gaza Nakba"), and Likud MKs Vaturi, Gotliv, Distel-Atbaryan, Bismuth, Kallner, Revivo, Shitrit and Saada. Almost all of them appear in South Africa's ICJ application or in the statement list that accompanied it. Later statements in 2024–2025 (May Golan, Milwidsky, Vaturi's "subhuman" remarks, Halevi) are documented mainly in secondary reporting. Sanctions were almost nonexistent. The Knesset Ethics Committee cleared Vaturi, and the AG decided in Nov 2024 not to open criminal probes.

### Cited Findings

#### A. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu
**A1. "Remember what Amalek has done to you": televised address, 28 Oct 2023 [V1]**
- English (official translation as given): "You must remember what Amalek has done to you, says our Holy Bible. And we do remember and we are fighting. Our brave troops … are joining this chain of Jewish heroes … Our hero troops, they have one supreme main goal: to completely defeat the murderous enemy and to guarantee our existence in this country… Never again is now." Video: youtube.com/watch?v=lIPkoDk6isc. — [Al-Haq list, fn 2](https://www.alhaq.org/cached_uploads/download/2024/01/10/official-translation-of-statements-informal-translations-final-list-1704917626.pdf)
- Context: delivered at the start of the ground operation in Gaza.

**A2. Letter to soldiers and officers, published on @IsraeliPM_heb, 3 Nov 2023 [V1]**
- Hebrew (reconstructed): "מאז ומעולם נאבקנו באויבים מרים, שקמו עלינו לכלותנו. כשאנו מצוידים בעוז רוח ובצדקת הדרך, עמדנו בנחישות מול מבקשי נפשנו. המאבק הנוכחי במרצחי ה'חמאס' הוא פרק נוסף בסיפור העמידה הלאומית לדורותיה. 'זכור את אשר עשה לך עמלק' … זוהי מלחמת בני האור בבני החושך. לא נרפה משליחותנו עד שהאור יכריע את החושך – הטוב יביס את הרוע הקיצוני שמאיים עלינו ועל העולם כולו."
- English: "'Remember what Amalek did to you' … this is the war between the sons of light and the sons of darkness. We will not let up on our mission until the light overcomes the darkness — the good will defeat the extreme evil that threatens us and the entire world." Tweets: twitter.com/IsraeliPM_heb/status/1720406463972004198 and …1720406469055500583 — [Al-Haq list, fn 3](https://www.alhaq.org/cached_uploads/download/2024/01/10/official-translation-of-statements-informal-translations-final-list-1704917626.pdf)
- **Note for defense or context:** the Hebrew letter itself names the enemy as "מרצחי ה'חמאס'" (the Hamas murderers) in the sentence just before the Amalek line.
- Other Netanyahu statements cited by South Africa (§101 of the application, per a secondary coding of the application): "bloodthirsty monsters" (15 Oct 2023), "a struggle between the children of light and the children of darkness" (16 Oct 2023, Knesset), "striking our enemies with unprecedented might" (13 Oct 2023). — [Archive Genocide coded statements CSV (secondary)](https://archivegenocide.com/intent/statements.csv); [Al-Haq list fn 1](https://www.alhaq.org/cached_uploads/download/2024/01/10/official-translation-of-statements-informal-translations-final-list-1704917626.pdf)

**Netanyahu's own defense (Amalek):**
- Malcolm Shaw, counsel for Israel at the ICJ, argued that the full quotation ties Amalek to Hamas. Netanyahu's office called South Africa's charge "false and preposterous" and said it reflected "a deep historical ignorance". It noted that the verse appears at Yad Vashem and on a memorial in The Hague. — [Engineering News/Reuters, 17 Jan 2024](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/sas-amalek-genocide-complaint-is-historically-ignorant-says-israels-netanyahu-2024-01-17); [JNS](https://www.jns.org/netanyahu-slams-south-africas-amalek-slander/)
- Counter-view: critics (e.g., The New Arab) called the denial "unconvincing", given the verse's use by far-right groups. — [The New Arab](https://www.newarab.com/news/icj-decision-looms-netanyahu-denies-inciting-genocide)
- On 10 Jan 2024 Netanyahu issued a video statement: "Israel has no intention of permanently occupying Gaza or displacing its civilian population". — [Archive Genocide CSV, row C02 (secondary)](https://archivegenocide.com/intent/statements.csv)
- **Interpretation dispute:** the Amalek reference is legally contested. Jewish commentators read the command metaphorically, while South Africa and the UN Commission of Inquiry (COI) read it as evidence of intent. Present both readings.

**Consequences (Netanyahu):**
- The UN COI (16 Sep 2025) concluded that Netanyahu, Herzog and Gallant incited genocide and that Israeli authorities failed to act against them. It cited the Amalek comparison and the letter to soldiers. — [NPR, 16 Sep 2025](https://www.npr.org/2025/09/16/g-s1-89014/israel-gaza-genocide-un-inquiry); [UN press release](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/israel-has-committed-genocide-in-the-gaza-strip-un-commission-finds-16sep25/); [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2025/09/un-inquiry-finds-top-israeli-officials-incited-genocide-gaza)
- The ICC issued arrest warrants for Netanyahu and Gallant (Nov 2024), for alleged war crimes and crimes against humanity. These warrants concern conduct, not speech. — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2025/09/16/g-s1-89014/israel-gaza-genocide-un-inquiry)

## 2. Which statements were cited by South Africa at the ICJ, in the ICJ order, or by other international bodies?

### Takeaway
The ICJ's 26 Jan 2024 order relied only on Gallant (9 and 10 Oct 2023), Herzog (12 Oct) and Katz (13 Oct). South Africa's application (29 Dec 2023, §§101–107) cited a much broader set: Netanyahu (Amalek, "children of light/darkness"), Gallant, Katz, Dichter, Vaturi, and Likud MKs Gotliv, Distel-Atbaryan, Bismuth, Kallner, Revivo and Shitrit (§105). The UN COI (Sept 2025) found that Netanyahu, Gallant and Herzog incited genocide.

### Cited Findings
- ICJ order of 26 Jan 2024 (Section IV, paras. 35–59): cited "statements made by Mr Yoav Gallant, Defence Minister of Israel, on 9 and 10 October 2023", "by Mr Isaac Herzog, President of Israel, on 12 October 2023" and "by Mr Israel Katz, then Minister of Energy and Infrastructure of Israel, on 13 October 2023". It ordered Israel (16–1) to "take all measures within its power to prevent and punish the direct and public incitement to commit genocide in relation to members of the Palestinian group in the Gaza Strip". — [ICJ summary of the order](https://icj-cij.org/node/203454)
- The ICJ order paragraphs citing these statements are reported as ¶¶50–54 (secondary). — [Archive Genocide](https://archivegenocide.com/intent.html)
- South Africa's application §§101–107 (pp. 59–67, fn 439–506) lists "expressions of genocidal intent". §101 covers Netanyahu, Herzog, Gallant, Ben-Gvir, Katz, Smotrich, Eliyahu and Dichter. §105 covers Knesset members (Gotliv, Distel-Atbaryan, Revivo, Shitrit, Bismuth, Kallner, plus Kroizer, Lieberman and Ben-Ari). Vaturi's 7 Oct tweet is listed with the ministers' statements. — [NYU JILP note (Van Santen)](https://www.nyujilp.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Van-Santen_571-155-194.pdf); [Archive Genocide coded CSV (secondary, paragraph/footnote mapping)](https://archivegenocide.com/intent/statements.csv); [Al-Haq translations list](https://www.alhaq.org/cached_uploads/download/2024/01/10/official-translation-of-statements-informal-translations-final-list-1704917626.pdf)
- Israel's response at the ICJ (12 Jan 2024, CR 2024/2): the statements were "random, taken out of context or made by people without decision-making power", and official cabinet directives show an intent to limit civilian harm. — [ICJ CR 2024/2 as cited by Archive Genocide](https://archivegenocide.com/intent.html); [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/we-should-be-worried-israel-faces-peril-at-the-hague-in-gaza-genocide-case/)
- UN COI report (A/HRC/60/CRP.3, 16 Sep 2025): concluded that Netanyahu, Herzog and Gallant incited genocide. It cited Gallant's "human animals… act accordingly", Herzog's "entire nation" remark, and Netanyahu's Amalek comparison and letter to soldiers. It said statements by other leaders "should be assessed". Israel's Foreign Ministry called the report "distorted and false". — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2025/09/16/g-s1-89014/israel-gaza-genocide-un-inquiry); [UN](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/israel-has-committed-genocide-in-the-gaza-strip-un-commission-finds-16sep25/); [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/un-panel-claims-genocide-underway-in-gaza-israel-blasts-members-as-hamas-proxies/)
- ICC: arrest warrants for Netanyahu and Gallant (Nov 2024), for alleged war crimes and crimes against humanity (conduct, including starvation). These are separate from the speech cases. — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2025/09/16/g-s1-89014/israel-gaza-genocide-un-inquiry)

## 3. Were any of these statements investigated for incitement by the Israeli AG?

### Takeaway
On 9 Jan 2024 the AG and State Attorney warned that calls to intentionally harm civilians "may constitute criminal offenses" and announced "examinations". On 18 Nov 2024 the AG decided **not** to open criminal investigations into ministers and MKs (including the Distel-Atbaryan and Eliyahu statements) and gave no reasons. The only reported push for a probe concerned Ben-Gvir (not Likud), in July 2024. No Likud MK or minister is known to have been indicted. Knesset ethics proceedings (e.g., Vaturi) ended without sanction.

### Cited Findings
- Joint statement by AG Baharav-Miara and State Attorney Amit Aisman (Jan 2024): "remarks that call for, among other things, the intentional harm to uninvolved civilians contradict the current policy and may constitute criminal offenses." On 9 Jan 2024 (three days before the ICJ hearing) the AG's office said "examinations" had been opened into several comments. — [Times of Israel, 25 Nov 2024](https://www.timesofisrael.com/ag-decides-not-to-open-criminal-probes-into-senior-officials-for-gaza-incitement/); [ToI on rights-group demands](https://www.timesofisrael.com/rights-group-demands-probes-of-potential-genocide-incitement-in-light-of-icj-orders/)
- 18 Nov 2024 decision (disclosed 25 Nov 2024 in a response to the Israel Democracy Guard High Court petition filed in Aug 2024): no criminal investigations into ministers and MKs. Examples in the petition were Eliyahu's atomic-bomb remark and Distel-Atbaryan's "wiping Gaza off the face of the earth". No reasons were given. — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/ag-decides-not-to-open-criminal-probes-into-senior-officials-for-gaza-incitement/)
- Report that the State Attorney would expand its inquiry into lawmakers' incitement against Gazans. — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/report-state-attorney-to-expand-inquiry-into-lawmakers-incitement-against-gazans/)
- July 2024: State Attorney Aisman asked the AG to open a criminal probe into Ben-Gvir (Otzma Yehudit) for Gaza incitement (Kan 11 report). — [JNS](https://www.jns.org/report-state-prosecutor-seeking-criminal-probe-against-ben-gvir-over-gaza-incitement/)
- Adalah sent the AG 50 examples of alleged genocidal incitement and complained of "systematic ignoring". — [summary via search; Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/rights-group-demands-probes-of-potential-genocide-incitement-in-light-of-icj-orders/)
- Knesset Ethics Committee: rejected Cassif's complaint against Vaturi ("burn Gaza"). — [Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/n99p1dt3x)
- Netanyahu suspended Eliyahu (non-Likud) from cabinet meetings over the nuclear remark. This was the only government sanction found. — [Al-Haq fn 14 / Kan](https://www.kan.org.il/content/kan-news/politic/596470/)
- The ICJ order required Israel to "prevent and punish" incitement and to report within a month. The AG's report to the ICJ has not been made public. — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/ag-decides-not-to-open-criminal-probes-into-senior-officials-for-gaza-incitement/)

### Inferences
- The gap between the 9 Jan 2024 announcement (timed before the ICJ hearing) and the 18 Nov 2024 decision not to investigate is itself evidence for an argument that Israel did not comply with provisional measure 3. The State, however, would argue prosecutorial discretion and free speech, and MKs enjoy substantive immunity for speech in the Knesset.

### Gaps
- The outcome of the Israel Democracy Guard High Court petition after Nov 2024 was not found.
- I did not determine whether any specific Likud MK (Vaturi, Gotliv, Golan, Milwidsky) was the subject of a named "examination".
