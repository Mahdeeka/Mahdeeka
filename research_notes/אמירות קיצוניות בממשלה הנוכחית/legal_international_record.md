## 1. ICJ – South Africa v. Israel (Genocide Convention, Gaza)

### Takeaway
The Court's provisional measures order of 26 January 2024 quoted three Israeli officials: Defence Minister Gallant, President Herzog and then-Energy Minister Israel Katz. Of these, only Gallant and Katz were ministers in the Netanyahu government; Herzog is the head of state. The order directed Israel to "prevent and punish the direct and public incitement to commit genocide." The case is now in the written merits phase. Israel filed its Counter-Memorial on 12 March 2026. A 21 May 2026 order set South Africa's Reply for 22 November 2027 and Israel's Rejoinder for 22 May 2029, so no merits judgment is expected before 2029–2030.

### Cited Findings
**South Africa's Application (29 December 2023)**
- The Application cited statements by Netanyahu ("Remember what Amalek has done to you"), Herzog ("It's an entire nation out there that is responsible") and Gallant ("fighting human animals"). It also cited statements by military officials and by soldiers. [V] — [Wikipedia, South Africa's genocide case against Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa's_genocide_case_against_Israel); primary document: [ICJ Application instituting proceedings, 29 Dec 2023](https://www.icj-cij.org/sites/default/files/case-related/192/192-20231228-app-01-00-en.pdf)
- [K] The statements appear in Part IV, section headed "Expressions of genocidal intent against the Palestinian people by Israeli State officials and others" (approx. paras 101–107). Beyond the speakers above, the section includes:
  - Netanyahu's Amalek references of 28 October and 3 November 2023
  - Gallant's "complete siege … no electricity, no food, no fuel" (9 October 2023)
  - Israel Katz ("They will not receive a drop of water or a single battery until they leave the world")
  - Ben-Gvir and Smotrich
  - Heritage Minister Amichai Eliyahu's nuclear-option remark
  - Agriculture Minister Avi Dichter ("Gaza Nakba")
  - Coalition MKs, including Nissim Vaturi ("erase the Gaza Strip from the face of the earth"), Galit Distel-Atbaryan and others
  - **Paragraph numbers and the full speaker list must be verified against the PDF above.**
- Times of Israel reported that South Africa cited remarks by Finance Minister Smotrich, among others, in its filings. [V] — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/report-state-attorney-to-expand-inquiry-into-lawmakers-incitement-against-gazans/)

**Provisional Measures Order (26 January 2024)**
- [K] **Para. 52** opens with "The Court has also taken note of a number of statements made by senior Israeli officials. It draws attention, in particular, to the following examples". It then quotes:
  - **Gallant, 9 October 2023:** he had ordered a "complete siege" of Gaza City, with "no electricity, no food, no fuel" and "everything [was] closed".
  - **Gallant, the following day (10 October 2023), to troops:** "I have released all restraints … You saw what we are fighting against. We are fighting human animals. This is the ISIS of Gaza … Gaza won't return to what it was before. There will be no Hamas. We will eliminate everything. If it doesn't take one day, it will take a week, it will take weeks or even months, we will reach all places."
  - **Herzog, 12 October 2023:** "… It is an entire nation out there that is responsible. It is not true this rhetoric about civilians not aware, not involved. It's absolutely not true … and we will fight until we break their backbone."
  - **Katz (then Minister of Energy and Infrastructure), on X, 13 October 2023:** "We will fight the terrorist organization Hamas and destroy it. All the civilian population in [G]aza is ordered to leave immediately. We will win. They will not receive a drop of water or a single battery until they leave the world."
  - Primary document: [ICJ Order of 26 Jan 2024 (PDF)](https://www.icj-cij.org/sites/default/files/case-related/192/192-20240126-ord-01-00-en.pdf)
- The Court referred to the Israeli Attorney General's January 2024 statement on incitement. [V] — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/report-state-attorney-to-expand-inquiry-into-lawmakers-incitement-against-gazans/); [ARTICLE 19](https://www.article19.org/resources/israel-icj-measures-incitement-genocide-preserve-evidence/)
- [K] Para. 79 notes the Attorney General's statement, which the Court summarised as saying that calls to intentionally harm civilians "may amount to a criminal offence, including the offence of incitement". The Court nonetheless considered a specific measure on incitement necessary.
- [K] **Operative para. 86(3):** "The State of Israel shall take all measures within its power to prevent and punish the direct and public incitement to commit genocide in relation to members of the Palestinian group in the Gaza Strip."
  - Adopted 16–1, with Judge Sebutinde against. Judge ad hoc Barak voted in favour of this measure and of the humanitarian-aid measure.
  - Para. 86(6) required Israel to report within one month.
  - [V] Votes on the six measures were 15–2, 15–2, 16–1, 16–1, 15–2 and 15–2. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa's_genocide_case_against_Israel)

**Later orders and filings**
- **28 March 2024:** unanimous order to ensure food supplies; the Court noted famine "is setting in". [V] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa's_genocide_case_against_Israel)
- **24 May 2024:** order to halt the Rafah offensive, 13–2. [V] — same source
- **South Africa's Memorial, 28 October 2024:**
  - Not public. More than 750 pages of text and more than 4,000 pages of exhibits.
  - South Africa said it proves genocidal intent, including from officials' statements and from incitement left "unchecked and unpunished".
  - [V] — same source; the Memorial's specific paragraphs on statements are not public.
- **Time-limit extensions for the Counter-Memorial:**
  - 14 April 2025: extended to 12 January 2026.
  - 20 October 2025: extended to 12 March 2026. [V] — [ICJ, Order of 20 Oct 2025](https://www.icj-cij.org/node/205931); [ICJ on X](https://x.com/CIJ_ICJ/status/1981289824247070998); [UN A/80/4, ICJ annual report](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/icj-report-2024-25-01aug25/)
- **Israel's Counter-Memorial** was filed on 12 March 2026. Israel's MFA announced it on 14 March 2026 and South Africa's presidency confirmed it on 15 March. [V] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa's_genocide_case_against_Israel); [UNISPAL – Order fixing time-limits: Reply and Rejoinder](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/icj-court-order-on-fixing-of-time-limits-reply-and-rejoinder-south-africa-v-israel/)
- **Order of 21 May 2026:** South Africa's Reply due 22 November 2027; Israel's Rejoinder due 22 May 2029. [V, secondary sources only] — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa's_genocide_case_against_Israel); [UNISPAL](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/icj-court-order-on-fixing-of-time-limits-reply-and-rejoinder-south-africa-v-israel/)

### Inferences
- Because the Court itself quoted Gallant and Katz, these are the strongest citable "judicial notice" items. Katz later became Defence Minister, which makes his statement especially relevant.
- Para. 86(3) creates a continuing obligation to "prevent and punish". The Attorney General's November 2024 decision not to investigate (section 6) is the obvious domestic counterpoint that South Africa can invoke at the merits stage.
- No merits ruling is possible before the 2029 Rejoinder, so the case remains pending as of November 2026.

### Gaps
- The exact paragraph numbers of the Application's statements section and the full list of quoted speakers could not be fetched (ICJ site 403). Verify against the PDF.
- The Memorial and Counter-Memorial are confidential. Their treatment of statements is unknown.
- No 2026 hearing, new provisional-measures request or intervention developments were confirmed in this research.

## 2. ICC – arrest warrants for Netanyahu and Gallant

### Takeaway
On 21 November 2024, Pre-Trial Chamber I issued warrants for Netanyahu and Gallant. The charges are the war crime of starvation as a method of warfare and the crimes against humanity of murder, persecution and other inhumane acts (8 October 2023 – at least 20 May 2024). The warrants themselves are classified, and the public press release does not quote ministers' statements. Israel's challenges have so far failed to suspend or withdraw the warrants, and they remain in force. The jurisdiction question was remanded to the Pre-Trial Chamber in April 2025, and no final ruling on it was confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- [K] The ICC press release of 21 November 2024 states:
  - The Chamber found "reasonable grounds to believe that each individual bears criminal responsibility for the following crimes as co-perpetrators for committing the acts jointly with others: the war crime of starvation as a method of warfare; and the crimes against humanity of murder, persecution, and other inhumane acts."
  - It also found responsibility "as civilian superiors for the war crime of intentionally directing an attack against the civilian population."
  - The period covered is "from at least 8 October 2023 until at least 20 May 2024".
  - The warrants are classified "secret" to protect witnesses.
  - Source: [ICC press release, 21 Nov 2024](https://www.icc-cpi.int/news/situation-state-palestine-icc-pre-trial-chamber-i-rejects-state-israels-challenges) (site returned 403 to the tool; verify wording).
- [K] Prosecutor Karim Khan's application statement of 20 May 2024 described the deprivation of food, water, electricity and fuel. The statements quoted in the ICJ order, such as Gallant's "complete siege", are widely understood to be among the underlying evidence, but the public ICC documents do not list ministerial quotes.
- **Appeals Chamber, April 2025:** reversed the Pre-Trial Chamber's dismissal of Israel's jurisdictional challenge as premature and remanded it, while refusing to suspend the warrants. [V] — [Opinio Juris, 25 Apr 2025](https://opiniojuris.org/2025/04/25/the-appeals-chamber-decides-israels-appeals-and-refuses-to-suspend-the-arrest-warrants/); [Jewish News](https://www.jewishnews.co.uk/icc-allows-israel-to-raise-jurisdictional-objections-to-netanyahu-arrest-warrants/)
- **Pre-Trial Chamber, July 2025:** rejected Israel's request to withdraw the warrants pending the jurisdiction challenge. [V] — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/icc-rejects-israeli-appeal-against-arrest-warrants-for-netanyahu-and-gallant/)
- **December 2025:** the Appeals Chamber reportedly dismissed Israel's challenge to the validity of the investigation. [V, weak sourcing] — [WAFA](https://english.wafa.ps/Pages/Details/156801); [PressTV, 15 Dec 2025](https://www.presstv.co.uk/Detail/2025/12/15/760710/ICC-rejects-Israel-s-appeal-to-invalidate-Netanyahu-s-arrest-warrant); [Al-Haq](https://www.alhaq.org/advocacy/26354.html). Verify on icc-cpi.int.

### Inferences
- For an Israeli lawyer, the ICC record links ministers' "siege" statements to the starvation charge only by inference. Unlike the ICJ order, the public ICC documents do not quote ministerial statements.

### Gaps
- No confirmed 2026 ICC ruling on the jurisdiction challenge was found.
- No confirmed new warrants (for example against Ben-Gvir or Smotrich) were found.
- Status of the Prosecutor (Khan's leave since May 2025) and of US sanctions on ICC officials in 2026 was not verified.

## 3. UN Commission of Inquiry (Pillay) and special rapporteurs

### Takeaway
The COI's 16 September 2025 report (A/HRC/60/CRP.3) found that Israel committed genocide in Gaza. It specifically concluded that President Herzog, PM Netanyahu and then-Defence Minister Gallant "have incited the commission of genocide" and that Israel failed to punish this. It said Ben-Gvir and Smotrich "should be assessed" for incitement.

### Cited Findings
- **Incitement finding:** "The Commission also concluded that Israeli President Isaac Herzog, Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and then Defence Minister Yoav Gallant, have incited the commission of genocide and that Israeli authorities have failed to take action against them to punish this incitement." [V] — [UNISPAL – A/HRC/60/CRP.3](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/commission-of-inquiry-report-genocide-in-gaza-a-hrc-60-crp-3/); [UNISPAL – OHCHR press release, 16 Sep 2025](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/israel-has-committed-genocide-in-the-gaza-strip-un-commission-finds-16sep25/)
- **Scope and other officials:** the report covers 7 October 2023 – 31 July 2025. It names Ben-Gvir and Smotrich as officials whose statements should be assessed for incitement to genocide. It does not determine individual criminal responsibility under the Rome Statute. [V] — [Time](https://time.com/7317574/israel-gaza-genocide-united-nations-commission-inquiry-report/); [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/un-commission-of-inquiry-accuses-israel-of-genocide-in-gaza-and-urges-global-action)
- **Israel's response:** the MFA called the report "distorted and false" and called "for the immediate abolition of this Commission of Inquiry". [V] — [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/un-commission-of-inquiry-accuses-israel-of-genocide-in-gaza-and-urges-global-action)

### Inferences
- The COI is the only UN body found that has made a named finding of incitement to genocide against a sitting PM and president. That gives it the most direct quotation value, although it is not a judicial body.

### Gaps
- Exact paragraph numbers of CRP.3 that quote each official (e.g. Netanyahu's Amalek speech of 28 October 2023) were not retrieved.
- Special Rapporteur Francesca Albanese's reports (e.g. "Anatomy of a Genocide", A/HRC/55/73, March 2024) were not fetched in this session.
- No COI follow-up reports from 2026 were verified.

## 5. US – Executive Order 14115 (settler sanctions) and its revocation

### Takeaway
President Biden's EO 14115 (1 February 2024) authorised sanctions on persons undermining peace and stability in the West Bank. It was used against violent settlers and related entities, but never against ministers. President Trump rescinded it on his first day in office (20 January 2025), and OFAC removed the designations.

### Cited Findings
- **The order:** EO of 1 February 2024, "Imposing Certain Sanctions on Persons Undermining Peace, Security, and Stability in the West Bank". [V] — [White House archive](https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/briefing-room/presidential-actions/2024/02/01/executive-order-on-imposing-certain-sanctions-on-persons-undermining-peace-security-and-stability-in-the-west-bank/)
- **Designations under the order** included:
  - Mount Hebron Fund (April 2024)
  - Hashomer Yosh (August 2024)
  - Avichai Suissa and Eitan Yardeni (October 2024)
  - Amana (November 2024)
  - Neither Ben-Gvir nor Smotrich was designated.
  - [V] — [Wikipedia list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_sanctions_involving_Israel)
- [K] **Revocation:** EO 14148, "Initial Rescissions of Harmful Executive Orders and Actions" (20 January 2025), rescinded EO 14115. OFAC delisted the persons shortly afterwards. — [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/28/2025-01901/initial-rescissions-of-harmful-executive-orders-and-actions) (verify)

### Inferences
- Unlike the UK and allies, the US never moved from settler sanctions to minister sanctions, and since January 2025 it has actively opposed such sanctions (Rubio, June 2025).

## 6. Domestic Israeli treatment: Attorney General, Knesset, courts; comparison with Arab MKs

### Takeaway
In January 2024, days before the ICJ hearing, the Attorney General and State Attorney publicly warned that calls to harm uninvolved civilians may be criminal, including as incitement, and said examinations had been opened. In November 2024, responding to a High Court petition, the Attorney General decided **not** to open criminal investigations against ministers or MKs, finding it not in the public interest.

By contrast, the Knesset used its strongest tool, impeachment under the 2016 law that requires 90 votes, against Arab or Hadash MKs:
- Ofer Cassif in 2024 [K]
- Ayman Odeh in 2025: the House Committee approved it, but the plenum vote of 14 July 2025 got 73 votes and failed

### Cited Findings
**Attorney General**
- January 2024: the AG and State Attorney said that comments understood as calling for intentional harm to uninvolved civilians could be criminal offences, including incitement. [V] — [Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/h1tnwvjdp)
- 9 January 2024: the AG's office said examinations had been opened into several officials' comments. [V] — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/ag-decides-not-to-open-criminal-probes-into-senior-officials-for-incitement-against-gazans/)
- The State Attorney reportedly expanded the inquiry after the ICJ order. [V] — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/report-state-attorney-to-expand-inquiry-into-lawmakers-incitement-against-gazans/)
- 18 November 2024 (reported 25 November): the AG accepted the State Prosecutor's position that investigating remarks by ministers and MKs about harming Gaza civilians was not in the public interest. The decision was made in response to a High Court petition. [V] — [Haaretz, 25 Nov 2024](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/2024-11-25/ty-article/.premium/ag-wont-investigate-remarks-by-israeli-ministers-mks-for-inciting-harm-to-gazans/00000193-63a4-d68e-a1db-eba48e5b0000); [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/ag-decides-not-to-open-criminal-probes-into-senior-officials-for-incitement-against-gazans/)

### Inferences
- The asymmetry is citable:
