## Q1. Bezalel Smotrich: criminal matters and extremist affiliations

### Takeaway
In summer 2005 Smotrich was detained by the Shin Bet for about three weeks during the anti-disengagement struggle. The case involved a seizure of 700 liters of fuel/oil and suspicions of conspiracy and road-blocking. His appeals against detention were rejected up to the Supreme Court (Justice Joubran), but **he was never charged** [DETAINED-NO-CHARGE]. He co-founded the NGO Regavim. A former Shin Bet deputy chief reportedly called him a "terrorist", which is an allegation and opinion, not a legal finding.

### Cited Findings
- **2005 detention [DETAINED-NO-CHARGE]:**
  - In July 2005 the Shin Bet seized 700 liters of fuel and oil from five right-wing activists. They allegedly planned to attack infrastructure or block roads to stop the Gaza evacuation.
  - Smotrich was among them and was held in a Shin Bet facility for "several weeks."
  - The suspicions were conspiracy to commit a crime, disrupting public transport routes, endangering human life, malicious damage, membership in a banned organization, and sedition.
  - The District Court rejected his appeal against detention. Supreme Court Justice Salim Joubran upheld that decision, writing: "The acts attributed to the appellants are serious, especially these days."
  - "No charges were ultimately brought against him." In 2015 he declined to discuss the arrest.
  — [Ynet, 2015](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/4614147) (also at [ynetnews.com/articles/0,7340,L-4614147,00.html](https://www.ynetnews.com/articles/0,7340,L-4614147,00.html))
- Wikipedia summary: "held for three weeks, uncharged, after being found with 700 litres of gasoline." It cites ToI on a former Shin Bet deputy chief who reportedly called Smotrich a "terrorist". Use the ToI link as the primary source; the "terrorist" label is an opinion or allegation. — [Wikipedia pointer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bezalel_Smotrich); [ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/former-shin-bet-deputy-chief-said-to-call-hardline-mk-smotrich-a-terrorist/)
- A 2025 Maariv opinion column by a former officer, Dvir Kariv, says he arrested Smotrich in 2005 and recalls the 700-liter seizure. This is a personal account and opinion, not a primary record. — [Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/israel/Article-1213615)
- **Regavim:** Smotrich is a co-founder of Regavim, an NGO that pursues legal action against unpermitted Palestinian and Bedouin construction. — [Wikipedia pointer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regavim_(NGO))
- **2006 "Beast Parade":** He helped organize a protest against the Jerusalem Pride parade and later expressed regret. In Feb 2015 he called himself a "proud homophobe" and later apologized. — [ToI on "proud homophobe"](https://www.timesofisrael.com/jewish-home-hopeful-boasts-of-being-proud-homophobe); [Wikipedia pointer for the parade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bezalel_Smotrich)
- **ICC:** Wikipedia says Smotrich hinted that the ICC was seeking an arrest warrant against him and threatened to evict Khan al-Ahmar in response. No primary URL was retrieved. — [Wikipedia pointer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bezalel_Smotrich)

### Inferences
- In advocacy texts, phrase it as: "detained for about three weeks by the Shin Bet in 2005 on suspicion of [offenses listed]; never charged." Do not write "arrested for terrorism" or "convicted."

### Gaps
- No court ruling text or Shin Bet statement on the 2005 case was retrieved. The Supreme Court detention-appeal decision (Joubran) could be located in Nevo or the Supreme Court archive.
- Exact founding year of Regavim and Smotrich's formal role there today were not verified.
- No primary source was retrieved on any ICC application concerning Smotrich.

## Q2. Smotrich: extreme and dehumanizing statements (chronological)

### Takeaway
Smotrich's documented statements run from 2016 through 2026:
- Arab–Jewish segregation in maternity wards (2016)
- the "Decisive Plan" (2017)
- "Arabs are citizens, for now at least" (2021)
- "Ben-Gurion didn't finish the job" (2021)
- "Huwara needs to be wiped out" (2023, retracted and apologized for)
- "no such thing as a Palestinian people" in Paris (2023)
- Gaza emigration and "100–200,000 not 2 million" (2023–24)
- "total annihilation"/Amalek (2024)
- starving Gaza "might be justified and moral" (2024)
- "Gaza will be totally destroyed" (2025)

These statements fed directly into Western condemnations and sanctions, and were reportedly referenced in South Africa's ICJ pleadings.

- Context: Smotrich, then a Jewish Home MK, reacted on Twitter to a radio report that hospitals were separating Jewish and Arab mothers on request. He backed the practice. He wrote that it was natural that his wife would not want to lie next to a woman whose baby "might one day want to murder his own baby," and that his wife "really isn't a racist" but did not want the celebrations customary in Arab families after a birth.
- Reactions: MK Yoel Hasson called it a "racist tweet." Party leader Naftali Bennett said he had gone too far. The Health Ministry said discrimination-based separation is prohibited.
- His wife Revital told Channel 10 she had "kicked an Arab obstetrician out" of the delivery room.
- No retraction found.

## Q3. International proceedings and foreign sanctions

### Takeaway
Smotrich, together with Ben-Gvir, was sanctioned on **10 June 2025** by the UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and Norway for inciting violence against West Bank Palestinians. Further measures followed:
- Slovenia and the Netherlands (July 2025)
- Spain (Sept 2025)
- Ireland (entry ban, 5 June 2026)
- France (entry ban, 9 June 2026)

Rothman's Australian visa was cancelled in Aug 2025. His statements were reportedly invoked by South Africa at the ICJ (May 2024 hearing per secondary sources), but I could not verify the primary ICJ documents.

### Cited Findings
- **UK, Canada, Australia, NZ, Norway (10 June 2025):**
  - UK designation under the Global Human Rights Sanctions Regulations 2020. Grounds: Smotrich is "responsible for engaging in, inciting, promoting and/or supporting activity which amounts to a serious abuse of the right of individuals not to be subjected to cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment, in particular acts of aggression and violence against Palestinian individuals in the West Bank."
  - UK measures: asset freeze, travel ban, director disqualification, imposed in a personal capacity.
  - Reporting says Norway and NZ imposed travel bans only.
  - Smotrich's response: "We are determined to continue building."
