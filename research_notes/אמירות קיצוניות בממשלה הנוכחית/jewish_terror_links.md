# Netanyahu's 37th government (Dec 2022 onward): documented links to Jewish terror and settler violence, and coalition statements supporting or excusing violence

> Research date: 22 Nov 2026. Legal-status labels used throughout: **CONVICTED**, **INDICTED**, **ARRESTED/DETAINED (no charges)**, **INVESTIGATED (closed)**, **STATEMENT** (documented public remark), **UNVERIFIED** (do not publish without primary-source check).
> Hebrew quotes marked "[HE – verify]" are the commonly reported Hebrew wording. I did not check them against the original audio or text in this session.

## Q0. Status of the coalition today: were the Oct 2026 elections held, and did the coalition change?

### Takeaway
Yes. Israel held elections on Tuesday 27 Oct 2026. Netanyahu's bloc won 53 seats, short of the 61 needed for a majority. On 8 Nov 2026 President Herzog gave Gadi Eisenkot (Yashar) the first mandate to form a government. As of ~21 Nov 2026, Netanyahu is still **caretaker** PM, so the Dec 2022 coalition remains the sitting (transitional) government. Otzma Yehudit and Religious Zionism both re-entered the Knesset.

### Cited Findings
- Election day was Tuesday 27 Oct 2026, and the final count was announced 29 Oct. Projected seats: Likud 27, Otzma Yehudit 7, UTJ 7, Religious Zionism 6, Shas 6; Yashar (Eisenkot) 21, B'Yachad 12, The Democrats 8, Yisrael Beytenu 7; Joint List 11 and Ra'am 8 (Arab parties 19, described as historic). Noam is not listed. — [Jerusalem Post, 29 Oct 2026](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-913237); [JPost bloc totals](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-913122)
- Herzog gave Eisenkot the first mandate on 8 Nov 2026 (28 days, extendable by 14). Two Arab-led parties endorsed Eisenkot. The new Knesset was sworn in on 10 Nov with Netanyahu as caretaker PM. Matan Kahana (Yashar) was elected Speaker on 17 Nov with 66 votes. — [SBS, 21 Nov 2026](https://sbs.com.au/news/article/israeli-election-latest-developments-explained/hu975eum9); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/israel/israels-final-election-results-show-neither-netanyahu-nor-rival-can-immedi-rcna606290)
- Coalition composition changed during the term:
  - Ben Gvir resigned on 19 Jan 2025 over the Gaza ceasefire and returned on 18–19 Mar 2025. — [Wikipedia: Itamar Ben-Gvir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itamar_Ben-Gvir)
  - Avi Maoz (Noam) quit as deputy minister in Feb 2023, and resigned from the government a second time on 24 Mar 2025. — [Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/rjczdkcrj); [Wikipedia: Avi Maoz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avi_Maoz)

### Inferences
- For advocacy published now, describe the government as "the outgoing/caretaker Netanyahu government (37th government)". Several of the individuals below were re-elected to the 26th Knesset (Otzma 7, RZ 6), but I did not verify the individual names on the new lists.

### Gaps

## Q1. Ministers and MKs with convictions, arrests or past involvement in Jewish extremist groups, and close relatives convicted

### Takeaway
**Itamar Ben Gvir** is the only sitting minister I found with a criminal conviction for supporting a terror organization (Kach) and for incitement to racism (2007). **Bezalel Smotrich** was detained by the Shin Bet in 2005 and released without charge. **Zvi Sukkot** was arrested in a 2010 mosque-arson probe and expelled from the West Bank in 2012, but I found no conviction. **Orit Strock's** son Zvi Struk was convicted in 2010 of kidnapping and abusing a Palestinian teenager. A former spokesman for **Limor Son Har-Melech** (Elisha Yered) was arrested in the 2023 Burqa killing.

### Cited Findings

**Itamar Ben Gvir (Otzma Yehudit; Minister of National Security)**
- **Kach background.** He joined the youth movement of Kach/Kahane Chai and became Kach's youth coordinator. By age 18 his record disqualified him from IDF service. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itamar_Ben-Gvir)
- **CONVICTED.**
  - In 2007 he was convicted of incitement to racism and supporting a terrorist organization (Kach). The conviction followed a protest after a Jerusalem bombing, where he chanted "Death to Arabs" and held signs reading "Expel the Arab enemy" and "Rabbi Kahane was right: The Arab MKs are a fifth column". — [Wikipedia, citing JPost 25 Jun 2007](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itamar_Ben-Gvir); [Times of Israel](https://timesofisrael.com/understanding-the-ominous-rise-of-israels-most-notorious-ultra-nationalist); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2023/8/27/who-is-israels-far-right-pro-settler-security-minister-ben-gvir)
  - NOTE: one Wikipedia reference dates the conviction to 2008. Check the verdict date against the court record.
  - A Times of Israel blog cautions that the conviction concerned propaganda for Kach, not participation in an attack. — [ToI blog](https://blogs.timesofisrael.com/itamar-ben-gvir-the-more-he-shocks-the-more-entrenched-he-becomes/)
- **Indictments.** By his own count in Nov 2015 he had faced 53 indictments, most of which were dropped. The New Yorker (Feb 2023) reports at least eight convictions in total. — [Wikipedia, citing Haaretz 2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itamar_Ben-Gvir)
- **Rabin (Oct 1995).**
  - He appeared on TV holding the Cadillac emblem taken from PM Rabin's car and said "We got to his car, and we'll get to him too" ("הגענו לסמל, נגיע גם אליו" [HE – verify]). Weeks later Rabin was assassinated by Yigal Amir. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itamar_Ben-Gvir); [Britannica](https://britannica.com/biography/Itamar-Ben-Gvir)
  - No source links him to Amir's plot.
- **Goldstein.**
  - He kept a portrait of Baruch Goldstein (perpetrator of the 1994 Cave of the Patriarchs massacre, 29 killed) in his living room until 2020. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itamar_Ben-Gvir); [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Itamar-Ben-Gvir)
  - Britannica says he dressed as Goldstein on Purim the year after the massacre and called him his hero. A 1995 video clip of the costume is cited by MEE and Electronic Intifada (advocacy-leaning). — [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com:443/biography/Itamar-Ben-Gvir)
  - In 2010 he was quoted saying Goldstein "protected his own people" (Yedioth, via counterextremism.com — secondary). — [CEP](https://counterextremism.com/extremists/baruch-goldstein)

**Bezalel Smotrich (Religious Zionism; Finance Minister and minister in the Defense Ministry)**
- **ARRESTED/DETAINED, NO CHARGES.**

The Shin Bet seized 700 liters of fuel and oil held by five right-wing activists, allegedly intended to block roads. He was released after several weeks without charge. — [Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/4614147)

- In the cases of Smotrich, Sukkot, Yered and Strock (personal), there was no conviction. They must be described precisely as "detained/arrested/expelled, not charged", or "staffer arrested". Struk's conviction is of the minister's son, not the minister.

## Q2. Coalition lawyers who represented Jewish terror suspects, and praise or defense of Goldstein, Amir, Duma, Huwara, Burqa, Jit and similar attacks

### Takeaway
Ben Gvir was the "go-to" lawyer for Jewish extremists, including two teenagers charged in the Duma arson. Coalition ministers and MKs repeatedly called for or excused collective punishment:
- Smotrich, on Huwara: "should be wiped out".
- Fogel, on Huwara: "closed and burnt". He was investigated and the case was closed.
- Ben Gvir, Eliyahu and Son Har-Melech defended the Burqa suspects in 2023.
- Smotrich wrote in 2015 that Duma was "not a terror attack".

## Q3. Government actions: administrative detention, US sanctions, international sanctions, legislation

### Takeaway
- Defense Minister Katz ended administrative detention for settlers (announced 22 Nov 2024) while keeping it for thousands of Palestinians, and freed the remaining settler detainees on 17 Jan 2025. The Shin Bet objected.
- Ben Gvir and Smotrich welcomed Trump's 20 Jan 2025 revocation of US settler sanctions.
- Five Western states sanctioned Ben Gvir and Smotrich themselves on 10 Jun 2025 for "inciting extremist violence".
- Slovenia and the Netherlands barred them from entry in July 2025.

### Cited Findings

**Administrative detention**
- **Fri 22 Nov 2024.** Katz announced the end of administrative detention for West Bank settlers: "it's inappropriate for the State of Israel to employ such severe measures against members of the settlement community."
  - Ben Gvir said it "corrects years of injustice and honors those who love this land".
  - Smotrich said it "ended years of discrimination against West Bank settlers".
  - Sources: [Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/b1uyv6pfje); [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/world/israel-announces-west-bank-settlers-no-longer-face-detention-trial-rcna181464); [Jewish Press, 22 Nov 2024](https://jewishpress.com/news/israel/settlements-israel/dm-katz-revokes-administrative-detention-of-jewish-settlers/2024/11/22)
- **Shin Bet position.** Bar had warned in June 2024 that banning the measure for Israelis "will result in an immediate, severe and serious harm to the security of the state." — [JPost](https://jpost.com/breaking-news/article-830265)
- **17 Jan 2025.** Katz freed the remaining settler detainees: "In light of the expected release of terrorists from Judea and Samaria as part of the hostage release deal, I have decided to release the settlers held in administrative detention."
  - ToI reported 16 orders were issued for Jewish Israelis under Gallant, 7 of them still held. INN reported 5.
  - Peace Now called it a move that "whitewashes and normalizes the rise of Jewish terrorism".
  - Sources: [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/ahead-of-palestinian-prisoner-release-in-gaza-deal-katz-says-hes-freeing-settlers-held-in-administrative-detention/); [JC](https://www.thejc.com/news/israel/israel-orders-release-of-settlers-under-administrative-detention-in-the-west-bank-c45z2lhm)
- One search summary claimed Katz "reversed within 24 hours" in Nov 2024. This is NOT confirmed by primary reporting; treat it as unreliable.

**US sanctions revoked (20 Jan 2025)**
- Trump rescinded Biden's EO 14115 (Feb 2024). Under it, 17 individuals and 16 entities had been designated in eight batches.
  - Smotrich called the sanctions "a severe and blatant foreign intervention in Israel's internal affairs".
  - Ben Gvir (who had resigned days earlier) called the revocation historic and said it "rights a great wrong".
  - Sources: [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/on-day-one-trump-rolls-back-biden-sanctions-regime-targeting-violent-israeli-settlers/); [JTA](https://www.jta.org/2025/01/21/israel/trump-lifts-sanctions-on-west-bank-settlers-earning-praise-from-the-israeli-right); [Dawn/AFP](https://www.dawn.com/news/1886751/ben-gvir-smotrich-welcome-trumps-decision-to-lift-sanctions-on-israeli-settlers)

**Sanctions against the ministers themselves**
- **10 Jun 2025.** The UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and Norway sanctioned Ben Gvir and Smotrich (asset freeze and travel ban) for "repeated incitement of violence against Palestinian civilians". The joint statement said they "have incited extremist violence and serious abuses of Palestinian human rights". The US (Rubio) condemned the sanctions. — [UK Gov press release](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-and-partners-unite-to-sanction-ministers-inciting-west-bank-violence); [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/middle-east/uk-canada-australia-others-sanction-2-far-right-israeli-ministers-inci-rcna212252); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/6/10/uk-and-allies-will-sanction-far-right-israeli-ministers-ben-gvir-smotrich)
- **17 Jul 2025.** Slovenia declared both persona non grata (the first EU state), citing "genocidal statements". — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/in-eu-first-slovenia-to-ban-ben-gvir-and-smotrich-over-genocidal-statements/)
- **Late Jul 2025.** The Netherlands barred both for repeatedly inciting settler violence and pledged to list them in the Schengen register. — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/netherlands-bars-entry-to-smotrich-ben-gvir)
- Per Wikipedia only, not independently verified:
  - Poland imposed a 5-year entry ban and France declared Ben Gvir persona non grata (May 2026), over the treatment of flotilla detainees.
  - Ireland imposed a travel ban on both (June 2026).
  - Turkey's chief prosecutor issued an arrest warrant (7 Nov 2025).
  - Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itamar_Ben-Gvir)

**Legislation**
- **30 Mar 2026.** The Knesset passed the Death Penalty for Terrorists law, 62–48, which Ben Gvir championed.
  - The text applies to a "resident of the area, except for an Israeli citizen or Israel resident". In practice this means West Bank Palestinians; Jewish perpetrators, who are Israeli citizens or residents, are excluded by design.
  - The UN human rights office called it discriminatory.

## Q5. Contrast data: Arab MKs' terror-related cases and Knesset expulsion attempts

### Takeaway
Reliable cases of Arab (ex-)MKs convicted of security-related offenses are few:
- **Basel Ghattas, 2017.** Plea deal: phone and document smuggling to security prisoners. The terror/aiding-the-enemy charges were **dropped**.
- **Said Naffaa, 2018.** Convicted of visiting an enemy state and contact with a foreign agent, from a 2007 trip to Syria.

Expulsion attempts under the 2016 "impeachment law" were both pushed by the coalition, and both failed: Ofer Cassif (Jewish, Hadash) on 19 Feb 2024 (85 votes) and Ayman Odeh on 14 Jul 2025 (73 votes); 90 votes are required. Contrast: Ben Gvir, a minister, has a conviction for supporting a terror organization.

### Cited Findings
- **Basel Ghattas (Balad/Joint List).**
  - Beersheba Magistrate's Court convicted him on 22 Mar 2017 under a plea deal: smuggling cellphones and documents to prisoners, and breach of trust. The state **dropped** the aiding-the-enemy and terror-accomplice charges.
  - Sentence: 2 years, with moral turpitude sought.
  - Sources: [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/court-convicts-ghattas-after-plea-deal-upheld/); [Algemeiner](https://www.algemeiner.com/2017/03/17/arab-knesset-member-reaches-plea-deal-to-serve-2-years-in-jail-for-security-offenses/)
- **Said Naffaa (former Balad MK).** Convicted of visiting an enemy state and contact with a foreign agent (a 2007 Syria trip in which he met a PFLP official). His immunity was lifted in 2011. — [Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/4507323); [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/ex-balad-party-mk-convicted-of-security-crimes/)
- **Hanin Zoabi.** Convicted (2021 plea) of fraud and forgery in Balad party financing. This was **not** a security offense. — [Jewish Press](https://jewishpress.com/news/israel/the-knesset/ex-arab-mk-hanin-zoabi-convicted-of-fraud-and-forgery/2021/10/05)
- **2016.** Ghattas, Zoabi and Zahalka were suspended by the Ethics Committee for meeting families of attackers. This was a disciplinary measure, not a criminal one. — [JTA](https://www.jta.org/2016/02/08/israel/3-arab-israeli-mks-suspended-for-meeting-with-terrorists-families)
- **Ofer Cassif (Hadash, Jewish).**
  - The House Committee approved impeachment 14–2 (Jan 2024) over his support for South Africa's ICJ case.
  - The plenum vote on 19 Feb 2024 failed with 85 of the required 90.
  - Separately, on 8 Oct 2026 the Jerusalem Magistrate's Court convicted him of aggravated assault on a Border Police officer (May 2022 incident). This is a non-security offense, and a new impeachment motion was signed by 85 MKs.
  - Sources: [Times of Israel, Feb 2024](https://www.timesofisrael.com/bid-to-oust-mk-ofer-cassif-over-backing-for-icj-genocide-case-falls-short-in-knesset/); [Times of Israel, 8 Oct 2026](https://www.timesofisrael.com/hadash-mk-cassif-convicted-of-assaulting-police-officer-during-2022-west-bank-clash/)

Coalition MKs heckled him. — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/motion-to-expel-odeh-from-knesset-fails-to-pass-amid-heckling-by-coalition-members/); [JNS](https://www.jns.org/israel-news/motion-to-impeach-arab-knesset-lawmaker-fails-to-achieve-supermajority); [Adalah](https://www.adalah.org/en/content/view/11337)

### Inferences
- A defensible line for advocacy: no sitting Arab MK has been convicted of a terror offense in recent Knessets. Ghattas's terror charges were dropped, and his conviction was for smuggling and breach of trust. Meanwhile, the coalition's Minister of National Security holds a conviction for supporting a terror organization. **Verify "no sitting Arab MK" against the full list before publishing.**
