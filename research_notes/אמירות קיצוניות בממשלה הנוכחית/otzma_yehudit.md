## Q0. Current status (as of 22 Nov 2026): did the elections take place, and who still holds office?

### Takeaway
Elections were held on 27 Oct 2026. Otzma Yehudit won 7 seats. Gadi Eisenkot received the mandate on 8 Nov 2026 and no new government had been sworn in. Ben-Gvir and the other Otzma ministers therefore still hold office in the outgoing (transitional) Netanyahu government. Analysts expect Otzma to end up outside whatever coalition forms.

### Cited Findings
- **Election result:** held 27 Oct 2026 for all 120 seats. Otzma Yehudit won 7 seats (5.74%, 326,793 votes, +1). Likud won 27, Yashar (Eisenkot) 21. The Knesset dissolved on 17 Jul 2026. — [Wikipedia: 2026 Israeli legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election); [Haaretz, 30 Oct 2026, "Ben-Gvir's Seven Seats"](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/elections/2026-10-30/ty-article/.premium/ben-gvir-and-smotrich-split-the-right-and-lose-seats-in-the-process/000001a1-8bda-d54e-a5e1-9bde05e60000)

- **Mandate to Eisenkot:**
  - President Herzog gave Eisenkot the mandate on 8 Nov 2026, with 67 recommendations including the two Arab-led lists. He has 28 days, extendable by 14, so roughly 6 Dec, or about 20 Dec with the extension.

  - Sources: [Wikipedia: Itamar Ben-Gvir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itamar_Ben-Gvir); [Wikipedia: Amihai Eliyahu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amihai_Eliyahu); [JNS](https://www.jns.org/israel-news/ben-gvirs-otzma-yehudit-leaves-israeli-government)

## Q1. Criminal record and extremist affiliations

### Takeaway
Ben-Gvir has a **final criminal conviction** (Jerusalem Magistrate's Court, 25 Jun 2007) for **incitement to racism** and **support for a terrorist organization (Kach)**. On appeal (Jerusalem District Court, 17 Sep 2008) one count was overturned and the final sentence was **200 hours of community service plus a 60-day suspended prison sentence**.
- By his own count he was **indicted 53 times** (2015 interview), and most charges were dismissed.
- Secondary sources say he was convicted on **at least 8 charges** overall, but I could not find an itemized list.
- No other Otzma MK has a criminal conviction that I could find. Fogel and Almog Cohen were investigated and both files were closed in April 2024. Kroizer is under police investigation (2026) on suspicion of assaulting police officers, and has **not** been indicted.

### Cited Findings: Itamar Ben-Gvir (איתמר בן גביר)

**2007 conviction, original trial (CONVICTED)**
- **Court and date:** a Jerusalem court (the Magistrate's Court, בית משפט השלום) convicted him on Monday 25 Jun 2007 of incitement to racism and support for a terrorist organization. — [UPI, 25 Jun 2007](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/2007/06/25/Israeli-convicted-of-inciting-racism/34651182778134/); [Ynet English, 2007](https://www.ynetnews.com/articles/0,7340,L-3417226,00.html); [JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel/ben-gvir-convicted-of-inciting-to-racism)
- **Count 1, incitement to racism:** carrying a sign reading "Expel the Arab enemy." [HE-verified] **"יש פתרון - לגרש את האויב הערבי"**. — [UPI](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/2007/06/25/Israeli-convicted-of-inciting-racism/34651182778134/); [INN Hebrew](https://www.inn.co.il/news/164023)
- **Count 2, support for a terrorist organization:** a poster reading "Rabbi Kahane was right, the Arab MKs are a fifth column." [HE-verified] **"הרב כהנא צדק, הח"כים הערבים הם גייס חמישי"**. — same sources
- **Other charges listed by INN (2008):**
  - Distributing posters titled "החכי"ם הערבים הם גיס חמישי ופצצת זמן מתקתקת".
  - Driving with a sticker reading "אין ערבים - אין פיגועים" and running a campaign under that slogan.
  - Distributing stickers reading "או אנחנו או הם".
  - Source: [INN, 17 Sep 2008](https://www.inn.co.il/news/179756)
- **Acquittals at trial:** acquitted of the "Death to the Arabs" chant (after a Jerusalem bombing) and of a radio remark that outlawing Kach was a joke. The court held these did not amount to support for a terror organization. — [UPI](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/2007/06/25/Israeli-convicted-of-inciting-racism/34651182778134/)
- **Maximum exposure:** 8 years in total (5 for incitement to racism, 3 for supporting a terror group). The prosecution said it would seek jail. — [UPI](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/2007/06/25/Israeli-convicted-of-inciting-racism/34651182778134/)
- **Trial sentence:** the Magistrate's Court imposed community service (שירות לתועלת הציבור). The prosecution had sought prison to be served as labour (מאסר בעבודות שירות). — [INN, 17 Sep 2008](https://www.inn.co.il/news/179756)
- **Case number:** not found (Nevo not accessed).

**2008 appeal, Jerusalem District Court (ביהמ"ש המחוזי בירושלים), 17 Sep 2008**
- **Ben-Gvir's appeal:** partly accepted. He was acquitted on the posters-against-Arab-MKs count, and the court found no proof that he possessed the stickers.
- **State's appeals:** the appeal against the radio-interview acquittal was rejected, and so was the appeal on sentence.
- **Final sentence (reduced):** **200 hours of community service and a 60-day suspended prison sentence.**
- **Court's reasoning:** there was no "קריאה ישירה וחד משמעית לאלימות" (direct, unambiguous call to violence), and his statements were at the upper limit of incitement offences.
- **Source:** [INN, 17 Sep 2008](https://www.inn.co.il/news/179756)
- **Caution:** the INN lead paragraph and its body are not fully consistent on which counts survived the appeal. Get the district court judgment from Nevo.

## Q3. Actions in office (police, prisons, guns, Temple Mount, settler violence)

### Takeaway
In office, Ben-Gvir:
- loosened gun licensing (230,000 new licenses by Aug 2025, by his office's figure);
