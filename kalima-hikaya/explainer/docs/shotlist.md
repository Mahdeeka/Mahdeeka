# كلمة وراها حكاية: shot list
72 s · 96 bpm (0.625 s/beat) · 116 beats · formats: 9x16 (primary, WhatsApp/Instagram), 16x9 (projector)
Music: synthesized tabla bed, D dorian (`audio/music.wav`) · VO: Palestinian-dialect narrator (`audio/script.txt`, 14 lines, ~135 words) · Arabic subtitles per phrase, burned in

**World:** a green felt table (Harmony green #016D47) seen from above, cream cards (#EFDDCE) dealt onto it. The five round colours of the app appear only as the word cards. The speech bubble from the app's logo is the recurring transition: it grows to fill the frame and the next scene appears inside it.
**Type:** Lalezar for the big words (the app's word cards), Readex Pro for subtitles and labels. The wordmark «كلمة وراها حكاية» comes from the app as artwork. Arabic is animated word by word, never letter by letter, so the letters stay joined.
**Real screens only:** every phone shot is a real screenshot of the app (`assets/shots/`).
